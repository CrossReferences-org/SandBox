## Data

The `json/` folder is an export from
[CrossReferences.org](https://crossreferences.org). 
See the [data repository](https://github.com/CrossReferences-org/bible-cross-references) for the format,
translation licences and correction process.

This snapshot was exported on 2026-09-12 and is not kept in sync.
Corrections belong in the data repository, not here.
